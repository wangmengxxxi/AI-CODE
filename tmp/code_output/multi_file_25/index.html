About UMKC

Founded in 1929, UMKC is Kansas City’s only public research university and an anchor institution of the University of Missouri System.

Academic Excellence

Home to 17 nationally ranked programs, including Conservatory of Music and Dance, School of Medicine, and Henry W. Bloch School of Management.

Urban Advantage

Students learn in a vibrant, diverse metropolis — with internships at Hallmark, Cerner, Sprint, and the Nelson-Atkins Museum — all within walking distance.

Inclusive Community

UMKC enrolls students from every U.S. state and over 90 countries. Our Office of Diversity and Inclusion leads campus-wide equity initiatives.

Academics & Research

Rigorous, student-centered learning grounded in inquiry, creativity, and real-world impact.

Undergraduate Programs

125+ majors across six academic schools — with high-impact practices like undergraduate research, study abroad, and service-learning built in.

Graduate & Professional Studies

70+ master’s, doctoral, and professional degrees — including MD, JD, PharmD, and DMA — delivered by nationally recognized faculty.

Research & Innovation

Over $45M in annual research expenditures; partnerships with NIH, NSF, USDA, and industry leaders advancing health, tech, and urban policy.

Student Life & Support

A welcoming, connected campus where every student thrives — academically, socially, and personally.

Global Support Center

Dedicated advising, immigration counseling, orientation, and cultural programming for international students.

Academic Success

Tutoring, writing center, first-year seminars, and proactive advising ensure timely degree completion.

Wellness & Belonging

Counseling services, health center, LGBTQ+ Resource Center, and interfaith spaces foster holistic well-being.
