Our Urban Mission

More Than a University — A Civic Partner

Founded in 1933 as the first municipal university in Missouri, UMKC embraces its role at the heart of Kansas City. We collaborate with local businesses, nonprofits, hospitals, and arts institutions to drive innovation, equity, and economic vitality across the metro region.

  • 📍 Located in the vibrant Crossroads Arts District & Hospital Hill
  • 🎓 16,000+ students from all 50 states and 80+ countries
  • 🏆 Top 10% nationally for social mobility (U.S. News)

Excellence in Learning & Discovery

Honors College

Small seminars, faculty mentorship, and early research opportunities — ranked among the nation’s top public honors programs.

Health Sciences

Home to the School of Medicine, School of Dentistry, and School of Nursing & Health Studies — training future clinicians and researchers on the Hospital Hill campus.

Conservatory

Kansas City’s premier performing arts school — offering BFA/MFA degrees in music, theatre, dance, and art history with professional partnerships across the city.

Life in the Heart of KC

🌳 Green & Connected

Our 66-acre urban campus features LEED-certified buildings, walking trails, and direct access to streetcar, bike lanes, and downtown attractions.

👥 Inclusive Community

UMKC is a designated Hispanic-Serving Institution (HSI) and Asian American Native American Pacific Islander-Serving Institution (AANAPISI), with robust support centers including the DEI Office, LGBTQ+ Resource Center, and Veterans Success Center.

🎭 Culture & Innovation

From the Swinney Conservatory Concert Hall to the Miller Nichols Library Makerspace — creativity and critical thinking thrive here.
